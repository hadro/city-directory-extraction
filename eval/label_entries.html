You are validating entry_rate.py, the only instrument this project has for measuring fabricated people. Read the raw OCR line and decide what it really is. The predicted record is shown underneath so you can see what the model made of it.

You are deliberately not shown the metric's own verdict, or which part of the page the line came from. Both would anchor the judgement being collected.

The call

1 · entry
A real person listed in the directory — a name, usually an occupation, usually an address. “ Wm elk h 86 Laf av
2 · not an entry
Anything that is not a person's listing, however tidy the model made it look.
3 · unsure
Use it. Genuinely ambiguous rows are reported separately rather than forced into a class.

Notes

Judge the line, not the record. The model may have produced a neat-looking record from a line of advertising — that is exactly the failure being measured, and it counts as not an entry.

A business listing is not advertising. Keeping them apart is half the point: they are currently scored the same and they are different problems.
